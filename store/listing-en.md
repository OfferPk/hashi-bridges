# Play Store listing draft — Zen Bridges

## Title
Zen Bridges: Hashi Puzzle

## Short description
Offline Hashi (bridges) logic puzzle. Connect islands, relax, no wifi needed.

## Full description
Zen Bridges is a calm offline Hashiwokakero (Bridges) puzzle.

• Connect numbered islands with one or two bridges  
• Bridges never cross — classic Nikoli rules  
• 40 hand-checked levels from easy to hard  
• Hints, undo, and local progress  
• No account, plays fully offline  

Perfect for short brain breaks. Sound effects are optional.
