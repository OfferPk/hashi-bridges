Privacy Policy — Zen Bridges

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

Zen Bridges is an offline puzzle game. Progress and settings are stored only on your device (localStorage / app preferences).

  • No account, no login, no personal profile.
  • No analytics SDKs in the current build.
  • Optional ads (when enabled in a Play Store build) use Google AdMob with consent where required. Development builds use Google test ad units only.

Contact: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
